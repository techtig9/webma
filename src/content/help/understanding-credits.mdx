Every plan includes a monthly credit allowance that AI actions draw down. Credits reset each billing
cycle and don't carry over — unused credits don't roll into the next month.

## Monthly allowance by plan

- **Free** — 3,000 credits/month
- **Starter** — 10,000 credits/month
- **Pro** — 30,000 credits/month
- **Business** — 75,000 credits/month

## What costs credits

- Generate a full website — 2,500
- Generate from a reference URL — 3,000
- Regenerate completely — 750
- Generate a new page — 500
- Generate a new section — 200
- AI edit (one section) — 350
- Change theme — 100
- Voice prompt (transcription) — 50
- AI image generation — 600

## What's always free

**Exporting code and deploying to Vercel never cost credits**, regardless of plan — the credit system
only covers AI generation, not getting your finished site out of webma.

## If you run low

You'll get an email when your balance drops below 10% of your plan's allowance. If you run out before
your next reset, you can upgrade your plan or wait for the next billing cycle — either way, nothing
you've already built is at risk; you simply can't spend more credits until you have some again.

## A note on caching

An identical request (same action, same exact prompt) reuses a cached result instead of spending
credits again — so clicking "regenerate" twice in a row without changing anything is effectively
free the second time.
