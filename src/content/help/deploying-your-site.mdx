## Deploy to Vercel

From the generator's export bar, click **Deploy to Vercel**. This is available on Starter, Pro, and
Business plans (not Free) — deploying and exporting never cost credits either way.

By default, your site deploys under webma's own platform account. If you'd rather deploy under your
own Vercel account, connect it first from **Settings → Security** — once connected, deploys go there
instead, and you keep full control of the resulting project on Vercel's side.

## Before you deploy

If accessibility issues are found in your generated site (missing alt text, unlabeled form fields, or
similarly WCAG-relevant issues), you'll see a summary before deploying — you can review and fix them
first, or deploy anyway if you've decided they don't apply to your case.

## Export instead

If you'd rather host elsewhere, export your site as a ZIP, a plain React project, or a Next.js
project from the same export bar — no deploy step required, and again, no credits spent.

## Custom domains

See [Custom domains](/help/custom-domains) for connecting your own domain to a deployed site.

## Checking deployment status

After deploying, the export bar shows your deployment's status (queued, building, ready, or error)
and a link to view the live site once it's ready, plus build logs if something goes wrong.
