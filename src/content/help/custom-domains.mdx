## Plan limits

Custom domains are a paid-plan feature:

- **Free** — not available
- **Starter** — 1 custom domain
- **Pro** — 5 custom domains
- **Business** — unlimited custom domains

## Connecting a domain

From your project's settings panel, open the **Domains** section, enter the domain you want to
connect, and follow the DNS instructions shown there. Domain status moves through pending →
verifying → active as DNS propagates — this can take anywhere from a few minutes to a few hours
depending on your DNS provider.

## Troubleshooting

If a domain gets stuck in "verifying" for longer than expected, double-check that the DNS record
matches exactly what was shown when you added it — a common cause is a typo in the record value, or a
conflicting existing record for the same subdomain at your DNS provider.

## Removing a domain

You can disconnect a domain from the same Domains section at any time — this doesn't affect your
site itself, only where it's reachable from.
